## New finding — quantized server logprobs

Direct probes of the raw `/v1/completions` `logprobs:20` (two different
prompts) show every reported logprob at a position lies on an affine grid
`r_pos + k/16` (nats): a per-position residue (−0.00663, −0.00943) plus
exact 1/16 steps. In both campaigns' scoring rows, the label delta
`lp_A − lp_B` is always a multiple of 1/8 (0 odd-sixteenths over 76 rows on
holdout 4K), so JEV scores take a coarse set of values: 76 blocks → 43
distinct scores, max tie 6; ≈25–30 % of blocks tie exactly in every zone of
both cases. Ranking within a tie is arbitrary (pool order), so method
comparisons at the budget boundary are sensitive to this quantization.
Engine-side property (NInfer XL, rk4v4); not correctable from the prototype.
The 12-evaluation comparisons above are unaffected in direction (controls
lose regardless), but tie-heavy boundaries mean single-item differences
(e.g., JEV's `ctx-size-exclu` win on A 2K) carry limited weight.
